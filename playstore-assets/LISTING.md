## Full Description (4000 characters max)

PhysioCare Manager is the complete practice management app designed exclusively for physiotherapists. Manage your entire clinic from your phone — patients, attendance, billing, and reports — all in one simple app.

🏥 DESIGNED FOR PHYSIOTHERAPISTS
Built specifically for solo physiotherapy practitioners who need a simple, fast, and reliable way to manage their practice. No complicated setup, no learning curve.

👥 PATIENT MANAGEMENT
• Add patients with complete details — diagnosis, referring doctor, condition
• Set individual per-session charges for each patient
• Track treatment status — Active, Completed, or Dropped
• Search patients instantly by name, phone, or condition
• Filter by Active, Completed, or patients with pending dues

📅 ONE-TAP ATTENDANCE
• Mark attendance in one tap — Present, Absent, or Cancelled
• Today's dashboard shows all expected patients
• Only Present sessions are billed (never absent/cancelled)
• Color-coded monthly calendar for each patient (Green/Red/Grey)

💰 SMART BILLING & PAYMENTS
• Automatic billing — sessions × per-session rate
• Record payments in Cash, UPI, Card, or Other
• Partial payments automatically reduce the oldest dues
• See outstanding balance at a glance
• Per-session charge override for discounts or home-visit charges

📄 PDF BILLS VIA WHATSAPP
• Generate professional PDF bills for any month
• Bills show patient details, sessions attended, charges, payments, and balance
• Share directly via WhatsApp with one tap
• Your clinic name appears on every bill

📊 MONTHLY REPORTS
• See total sessions, billed amount, collected amount, and pending dues
• Per-patient breakdown for every month
• Export reports as CSV for accounting
• Track your income growth month over month

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• 100% offline — works without internet
• All data stored securely on your device
• Optional PIN-based app lock
• No data collection, no analytics, no ads
• Full backup and restore support

🎯 BUILT FOR INDIAN CLINICS
• Currency in ₹ (INR) with Indian number formatting (₹1,20,000)
• UPI payment mode support
• WhatsApp integration for bills
• Works on affordable Android phones

Whether you're just starting your practice or managing an established clinic, PhysioCare Manager helps you stay organized, reduce paperwork, and focus on what matters most — your patients.

Download now and transform how you manage your physiotherapy practice!
