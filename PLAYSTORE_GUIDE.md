## 📱 STEP 1: Get Your App Files

### From GitHub Actions:
1. Go to **https://github.com/ansarizunaid806-byte/PhysioCareManager/actions**
2. Tap the latest build (top one)
3. Scroll to **Artifacts** section
4. Download TWO files:
   - **PhysioCareManager-Debug-APK** → For testing on your phone
   - **PhysioCareManager-PlayStore-AAB** → For Play Store upload

> The AAB file is what Play Store needs. The APK is for your personal use.

---

## 🛒 STEP 2: Create Google Play Developer Account

1. Go to **https://play.google.com/console/signup**
2. Sign in with your Gmail
3. Pay the **$25 one-time registration fee** (credit/debit card)
4. Accept the developer distribution agreement
5. Verify your identity (may take 1-2 days)
6. ✅ Your developer account is ready!

---

## 📝 STEP 3: Create Your App Listing

1. Go to **https://play.google.com/console**
2. Click **"Create app"**
3. Fill in:

```
App name:        PhysioCare Manager - Patient & Billing
Default language: English (India)
App or Game:     App
Free or Paid:    Free (you can change later)
```

4. Click **"Create app"**

---

## 📄 STEP 4: Fill Store Listing

Go to **"Store listing"** in the left menu:

### App Details:
```
Short description (80 chars):
"Manage patients, sessions, payments & bills. Built for physiotherapists."

Full description:
(Copy from playstore-assets/LISTING.md in this project)
```

### Graphics:
```
App icon:          Upload playstore-assets/icon-512.png (512x512 PNG)
Feature graphic:   Upload playstore-assets/feature-graphic.png (1024x500 PNG)
Phone screenshots: Take 2-8 screenshots from your phone (see below)
```

### How to take screenshots:
1. Install the debug APK on your phone
2. Open each screen and take screenshots:
   - Dashboard (main screen)
   - Patient list
   - Attendance marking
   - Patient profile with calendar
   - Payment recording
   - PDF bill
3. Transfer screenshots to your computer or use Google Photos

### Category:
```
Category:     Medical
Tags:         physiotherapy, patient management, billing, attendance
```

---

## 🔒 STEP 5: Privacy Policy

1. In the left menu, go to **"App content"**
2. Find **"Privacy policy"**
3. Click **"Start declaration"**
4. For the privacy policy URL, you can use:
   - A free privacy policy generator: https://www.privacypolicygenerator.info
   - Or host the policy text from this project on any free website
   - Or use Google Sites (free): Create a page with the privacy policy text

---

## 📊 STEP 6: App Content Rating

1. In **"App content"** → **"Content rating"**
2. Click **"Start questionnaire"**
3. Answer questions:
   - Does your app contain violence? → **No**
   - Does it contain sexual content? → **No**
   - Does it collect user data? → **No**
   - Is it connected to social networks? → **No**
4. Save → You'll get a rating of **"Everyone"**

---

## 🎯 STEP 7: Target Audience

1. In **"App content"** → **"Target audience"**
2. Select: **"18 and older"** (since it's a professional tool)
3. Confirm it's not directed at children

---

## 📱 STEP 8: Upload Your App

1. Go to **"Production"** in the left menu
2. Click **"Create new release"**
3. Upload the **AAB file** (PhysioCareManager-PlayStore-AAB from GitHub)
4. Add release notes: "Initial release - Complete physiotherapy practice management"
5. Click **"Next"** → **"Save"** → **"Send for review"**

---

## ⏳ STEP 9: Wait for Review

- Google typically reviews apps in **1-7 days**
- You'll get an email when it's approved or if changes are needed
- Common reasons for rejection:
  - Missing privacy policy
  - Missing screenshots
  - App crashes (unlikely for our app!)
  - Misleading description

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "App rejected"
- Read the rejection email carefully
- Fix the specific issue
- Resubmit (usually approved next time)

### "Your account was terminated"
- Very rare for legitimate apps
- Contact Google support
- Ensure you followed all policies

### "No downloads"
- Improve your screenshots
- Add more keywords in description
- Ask friends to download and review
- Share on social media
